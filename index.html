Enzyme Inactivation Optional
Skip this step for microalgae samples.
  1. Set the water bath in Room D11.1-3 (Patrick's lab) to 85 °C and allow approx. 30 min to stabilize.
  2. Verify the temperature using the pH meter probe from the Chem lab.
  3. Place food samples in the water bath for 15 min, then transfer immediately onto ice.
Centrifugation Optional
Full digest ≈ 3/8 of total digest volume
Supernatant ≈ 5/8 of total digest volume
  1. Prepare labeled containers in advance (full digest, supernatant, pellet).
  2. Pre-cool the large centrifuge in the microbiology lab to 4 °C (approx. 15 min).
  3. After intestinal incubation (2 h), stop shaking and aliquot approx. 3/8 of each sample into full-digest tubes.
  4. Centrifuge the remaining fraction for 30 min at 10,000 × g, 4 °C. Balance the centrifuge properly.
  5. Transfer the supernatant to labeled containers; keep the pellet in the digestion tube.
  6. Freeze all fractions: tape together corresponding full digest, supernatant, and pellet tubes. Label with name, date, and content. Store at −20 °C (short-term) or −80 °C (long-term).
